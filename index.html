reel-factory

A personal tool that makes short educational videos for one YouTube channel.

What it does

reel-factory is a private script that runs on its owner's own computer. It researches free, open-source AI tools and everyday technology topics (from public sources such as GitHub and Wikipedia), writes a short script, renders a vertical video with code-drawn graphics and an AI-generated voiceover, checks the result, and uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it uses Google sign-in

The owner signs in with Google once to let the tool upload videos to their own YouTube channel and read that channel's video statistics. The tool is not offered to anyone else and does not access any other user's account.
