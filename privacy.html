Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

reel-factory ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to make and upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses

  • Google account access you grant: permission to upload videos to, and manage videos on, your YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube), and optionally to read your channel's analytics (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Channel and video statistics: view, like and comment counts (and, if enabled, watch-time percentages) of videos the app uploaded, used only to improve future videos.

How the data is stored

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the app's local folder. The app has no server and no database of its own.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share your Google user data with anyone. Data is sent only to Google's own APIs to perform the actions you authorized (uploading videos and reading their statistics). Video scripts are generated with Google's Gemini API from public research sources; no Google user data is sent to it.

Limited use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the app's local folder to delete all stored data.
